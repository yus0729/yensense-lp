Philosophy

We use it first.

Built by us, used by us.

We plan, build, and operate every product ourselves.

We do not try to transform the world all at once through grand systems. We work on smaller worlds, improving them a little every day. We use what we build ourselves and release the things that make the world—even slightly—better. What matters most to us is that the people who choose our products will still want to use them next year and for many years after.

Origin

Connections become circles.

En becomes yen. Value comes around.

We live in an age when AI can generate answers to almost anything and social media constantly reshapes what people value. Life is more convenient, yet we increasingly follow choices made by others instead of deciding for ourselves what is worth choosing.

That is why we believe the greatest value ahead lies not in the amount of information we have, but in our own “Sense”—the ability to recognize what matters, to feel, and to choose.

Yen and en.

The meaning behind our name.

The “Yen” in our name carries two Japanese ideas: 円, a circle and the yen, and 縁, a connection between people. When we choose and act through our own Sense, we create connections with others. As those connections grow and we recognize the value in one another’s choices, scattered intentions come together to form a circle.

A connection forms and grows into a circle. It all begins with each person’s Sense.

Choosing through your own sensibility is more than an individual decision. It creates connections with others and turns those connections into a new cycle of value.

The circle we choose to draw.

What circle to draw.

We want to build a world where everyone can trust their own Sense, where each choice creates a connection, and where those connections eventually draw a larger circle through society.

In an age when AI can provide the answers, what people need is not more answers. We need the Sense to choose what to feel, what to believe, who to connect with, and what kind of circle to draw. That is the idea at the heart of YenSense.
